# Art Direction

## Target look
Premium cinematic light-novel illustration, not a raw anime screenshot and not photorealistic 3D.

Characters:
- instantly recognizable through face, silhouette, hair, outfit identity, proportions, and signature details;
- expressive enough for long dialogue scenes;
- integrated into lighting and environment rather than looking pasted over a background.

Environment:
- detailed fantasy architecture and props;
- strong depth and atmospheric perspective;
- coherent light direction;
- restrained bloom and depth of field;
- foreground/midground/background separation.

## Approved UI direction
- full-screen illustration;
- elegant lower-middle dialogue box;
- no permanent bottom control bar;
- small icon-only hide-UI control at upper-right;
- temporary arc/location/chapter treatment at upper-left.

## Composition policy
Use three asset classes:
1. Background.
2. Character/environment composition.
3. Hero CG.

Do not produce a new Hero CG for ordinary line-by-line changes.

## Emotional direction
When tone changes, prefer the smallest correct intervention:
1. expression change;
2. pose/focus adjustment;
3. shot/camera adjustment;
4. new composition;
5. Hero CG only when the moment earns it.

## Animation policy
Cinematic mode should create life, not spectacle.

Preferred:
- blink;
- subtle breathing;
- small hair/clothing secondary motion;
- slow camera push/pan;
- parallax;
- particles and weather;
- fire/smoke;
- light modulation;
- subtle foreground drift.

Avoid:
- rubbery warping;
- excessive camera movement;
- looping motion that distracts from reading;
- effects applied merely because the engine supports them.

If an image cannot be safely separated into layers, keep it intact and use global effects only.

## Return by Death visual language
Use a consistent project-owned visual identity:
- Subaru isolated as visual center;
- environment loses stability;
- saturation/lighting shifts;
- shadow-hand motif closes in;
- escalating audio/visual tension;
- blackout and checkpoint return.
